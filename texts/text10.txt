Cloud computing provides on demand access to computing resources such as servers, storage, and applications over the internet. It eliminates the need for physical infrastructure and reduces costs for businesses. Services are typically offered in models like IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS. Companies like Amazon, Microsoft, and Google provide cloud platforms. Scalability and flexibility are key advantages of cloud computing. Data can be accessed from anywhere with an internet connection. It has become an essential part of modern IT infrastructure and software deployment.
