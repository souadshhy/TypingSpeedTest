Software engineering is the discipline of designing, developing, and maintaining software systems in a systematic and efficient way. It involves following structured methodologies such as agile and waterfall models. Developers work in teams to build reliable and scalable applications. Requirements analysis, system design, implementation, testing, and maintenance are key stages of the software development lifecycle. Good software engineering practices ensure high-quality products that meet user needs. It also emphasizes documentation, version control, and continuous improvement throughout the development process.
