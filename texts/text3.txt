Computer networks allow devices to communicate and share resources efficiently across different locations. They are essential in modern computing environments such as businesses, schools, and homes. Networks can be wired or wireless depending on the infrastructure used. Key components include routers, switches, and servers. Data is transmitted using protocols that ensure reliability and security. The internet is the largest example of a global computer network connecting billions of devices. Understanding networking concepts is important for system administration, cybersecurity, and software development fields.
