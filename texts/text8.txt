Cybersecurity focuses on protecting computer systems, networks, and data from unauthorized access and attacks. It involves techniques such as encryption, firewalls, and authentication mechanisms. With the rise of digital systems, cyber threats have become more frequent and sophisticated. Organizations invest heavily in security measures to protect sensitive information. Ethical hackers test systems for vulnerabilities to improve security. Users are also advised to follow safe online practices. Cybersecurity is a critical field that ensures the safety and integrity of digital infrastructure worldwide.
